Marketing plan & advertising costs for sellers in Newcastle & the Hunter

Before you sign a selling authority, you should understand what a marketing plan usually covers and how advertising packages are often discussed relative to agent fees. This plain-English guide covers high-level marketing plan themes (photos/video, portals, social, print/signage, opens, copy, staging), how packages vs fees are commonly framed, transparency questions to ask at appraisal, five-council qualitative notes and FAQ — across Newcastle and the Hunter. It is not legal or financial advice and invents no dollar amounts, fee tables, package prices, commission percentages, view guarantees or “#1” rankings. Your agent proposes the campaign; your solicitor reviews the authority before you commit.

What a seller marketing plan usually covers

At a high level (education themes only — packages vary by agent and property):

  • Photography & video — stills, floor plans, walk-through or drone where appropriate; quality and timing relative to presentation.
  • Online portals — major listing sites and how your property is featured (basic vs featured placement themes — no invented reach claims).
  • Social & email — organic posts, paid social themes, database or email reach where used.
  • Print & signage — board, brochure or print ads where still used for your property type and pocket.
  • Open homes & private inspections — cadence, private vs open, and how enquiry is captured. See Open homes & private inspections.
  • Listing copy & price talk — how your home is described and how public price language is framed. See NSW price guides & underquoting.
  • Presentation / staging themes — declutter, styling or staging recommendations that support photos and opens. See Staging vs DIY · Preparing your home to sell.

A useful plan names channels, creative, timing and who pays for each line — not vague “maximum exposure” promises. Interview framing: How to choose a real estate agent in Newcastle.

Advertising packages vs agent fees (high-level)

Common qualitative discussion themes (not a fee schedule):

  • Separate line items — marketing/advertising is often discussed separately from commission or agency fees, though some agents package inclusions differently.
  • Fixed vs optional — some channels may be recommended as core; others optional upgrades. Ask what is mandatory vs discretionary.
  • When payable — before launch, during campaign, or on sale — confirm in writing before you sign.
  • GST presentation — how tax is shown on marketing and fee lines.
  • Apples-to-apples comparison — a lower headline fee with expensive mandatory marketing can cost more overall; compare full written outlines.

Transparency themes to insist on

  • Written campaign outline before you sign the selling authority.
  • Withdrawing a property from sale & changing agents
  • Clear ownership of who pays for photos, portals, social, print and boards.
  • What happens to unused or unused-upgrade spend if the campaign changes method (e.g. auction to private treaty).
  • How enquiry, open attendance themes and feedback will be shared with you.
  • That any deposit and settlement later run through lawyers and trust accounts — never as an online card payment on this site.

Questions to ask at appraisal

  • Can I see a written marketing plan for a similar property type in my pocket?
  • Which channels are core vs optional, and why for my home?
  • What is included in any “package” versus charged separately?
  • When are marketing costs payable, and are they refundable if we withdraw?
  • How do you report enquiry and open feedback during the campaign?
  • How should I compare your outline with another agent’s — same channels, same creative quality?

Five-council qualitative notes

Qualitative framing only — not performance claims, not invented spend benchmarks:

  • Newcastle — inner and near-city stock; creative and portal emphasis may reflect apartment vs house campaigns. Start with Selling in Newcastle.
  • Lake Macquarie — lakeside and hillside homes; lifestyle creative and open cadence often matter alongside portals. See Selling in Lake Macquarie.

FAQ

What does a seller marketing plan usually cover?

At a high level, plans often cover photography and video, online portal listings, social and email reach, print or signage where used, open-home cadence, listing copy, and presentation or staging themes — plus who pays for each line. Exact packages vary by agent and property. Education only — not financial advice.

Are advertising packages separate from agent fees?

Often yes in discussion: marketing/advertising line items are commonly talked about separately from commission or agency fees, though packaging and inclusions differ. Insist on clear written disclosure of what is included, optional or seller-paid. This site invents no dollar amounts or commission percentages. Confirm with your agent and solicitor.

What should I ask at appraisal about marketing costs?

Ask for a written campaign outline, which channels are recommended and why, what is fixed vs optional, when costs are payable, how GST is shown, and how results or enquiry feedback will be shared. Compare agents apples-to-apples. See our what-to-ask and selling-costs guides. Education only.

Do higher advertising spend packages guarantee more views or a higher sale price?

No guarantee. Reach and outcomes depend on property, price, presentation, method, competing stock and buyer demand — not spend alone. This site invents no view counts, ROI claims or performance rankings. Confirm expectations with your agent. Education only — not financial advice.

Who handles deposits and settlement if I list after agreeing marketing?

Any deposit and settlement run through lawyers and trust accounts — never as an online card payment on this site. Marketing costs and agency fees are separate commercial terms in your selling authority. Confirm with your solicitor. Education only — not legal advice.

Do marketing themes differ across Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Cessnock and Port Stephens?

High-level channel themes are similar, but buyer pools, property type and lifestyle positioning differ by pocket — so creative and channel emphasis may too. Use local agent advice; invent no medians, clearance rates or days-on-market averages. Education only.
