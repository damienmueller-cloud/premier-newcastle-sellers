What happens after an appraisal request

You submitted an enquiry. Here is the calm, honest sequence at Premier Estate Agents — so you know who contacts you, what to prepare, and that listing is always your choice.

1. Your enquiry reaches the Premier office

Appraisal requests from this seller site go to the directors and Premier Estate Agents office in New Lambton. Your details are not sold into a multi-agent lead auction. One enquiry, one office conversation.

2. Rodney, Vlado or the office contacts you

Someone from Premier will usually call to confirm timing, suburb and what you want from the appraisal. Prefer to speak first? Call Rodney on 0414 392 234 or Vlado on 0414 216 040.

3. A confidential appraisal — no obligation to list

The appraisal is a working conversation: likely buyer pools, comparable sales that actually fit your home, presentation priorities, and a method (auction or private treaty) that matches your timeline. You are not committing to list by requesting advice. If you do list later, settlement and any deposit are handled through your solicitor or conveyancer and trust accounts — never as an online card payment on this site.

What to prepare before the visit or call

  • Rough timeframe (researching, 1–3 months, ASAP).
  • Any known issues (repairs, strata, easements) you want discussed openly.
  • Questions on fees, marketing inclusions and who will be your day-to-day contact.
  • A shortlist of recent sales you have noticed nearby — useful for comparing how the agent uses comps.

If you have not heard back yet

Phone and email can miss each other. Call the numbers above, or resubmit via the appraisal form with a note in the message field. We would rather you feel looked after than left waiting.
