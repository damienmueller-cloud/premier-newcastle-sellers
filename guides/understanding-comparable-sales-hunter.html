Understanding comparable sales in the Hunter

Comparable sales (“comps”) are the backbone of a realistic appraisal. Here is how to read them without being swayed by stretch figures.

What makes a fair comparable

  • Similar property type, land size and condition — not just the same suburb name
  • Recent enough to reflect current buyer depth (ask how old each sale is)
  • Adjustments explained for views, renovations, corner blocks or flood/coastal factors

What to treat carefully

  • Offline or family sales that never tested the open market
  • One standout price that does not match the rest of the street
  • Asking prices from current listings — they are not sold evidence

Questions worth asking

  • Which three comps are you weighting most heavily for my home — and why?
  • What would push me to the top or bottom of your range?
  • How does campaign method (auction vs private treaty) change the pricing conversation?
